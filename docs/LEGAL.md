# Legal and distribution boundary

The public, canonical rights and licensing statement is
[RIGHTS_AND_LICENSES.md](../RIGHTS_AND_LICENSES.md). It defines the game-data,
screenshot, source, dependency, attribution, and package-distribution
boundaries for BrawlerPad.

`DEPENDENCIES.md` remains the engineering inventory of the exact pinned inputs.
It is not legal advice or a substitute for the license and notice files that
must ship with a binary.
