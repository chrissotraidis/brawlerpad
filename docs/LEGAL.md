# Legal and distribution boundary

BrawlerPad is an independent, unofficial source-port project. It is not
affiliated with or endorsed by Nintendo, HAL Laboratory, JRickey, Harbour
Masters, libultraship, or the upstream decompilation project.

No Nintendo ROM, extracted original asset, audio, model, texture, save, or
official logo may be committed, bundled, uploaded, attached to CI, included in
an app/IPA/DMG, or distributed by this project. Users must provide their own
legally obtained supported game data. BrawlerPad branding must use original
artwork and must not imply official status.

BattleShip's MIT grant is scoped to its port-owned code and expressly excludes
Nintendo/HAL material and the decompilation tree. The pinned decomp tree has no
explicit license file. That is a material distribution limitation: preserve
the upstream boundary and do not describe the entire combined tree as MIT or
fully open source. Written rights clarification is advisable before commercial
distribution or official-store submission.

HarkinianPad's owned integration material is all-rights-reserved unless a file
says otherwise. It is used here as a reference under the repository owner's
explicit direction; its notice does not relicense Shipwright or other third
parties. Adapt only the Apple-platform infrastructure relevant to this project
and preserve attribution and notices.

All distributed source and binaries must carry the actual license texts and
notices for BattleShip port code, libultraship, Torch, SDL, fonts, controller
database, and every fetched dependency. `DEPENDENCIES.md` is an engineering
inventory, not legal advice or a substitute for those texts.
